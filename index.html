What I paint

Three kinds of wall

Commercial

Clubs, restaurants and bars: 80+ sites across India and abroad. Every wall is new. Where the space allows, the piece includes glow colours, wooden installations or LEDs.

Residential

Kids' activity areas, mandirs, drawing rooms and home bars. A wall that carries your own idea instead of wallpaper picked from a catalogue.

Government

City beautification with the government: pillars, flyovers and abandoned sites painted with local legends such as Mohammad Rafi, and with messages for the environment.

About

Karuja Arts · since 2016

I was born in Punjab in 1991 and started painting walls professionally in 2013 in my hometown, Ludhiana. Since then I have painted over 200 murals and graffiti pieces in cities across India and abroad.

Most of the work is commissioned. Some of it is my own. Since 2016 I work with Karuja Arts, a team of artists with different specialities, taking on 6 to 8 projects every month.

I have also run hands-on art workshops in Ludhiana. Ask if you want one for your group.
